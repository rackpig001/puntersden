FAQ

Questions, answered

How do the AI agents work? +
Our AI agents pull in every NRL and AFL game — form, injuries, team news and the latest odds — and score each one against our tipping rules to find genuine value. Then a real punter reviews every tip before it goes out.
When do tips get posted? +
Tips go out midweek, before the first games of the round kick off, so you've got time to get set. The moment they drop we text the den — your private tips link lives on your phone, one tap away, any time.
What happens when a tip loses? +
It goes on the record, same as the wins. No tipping service wins every bet — anyone who claims otherwise is lying. We aim for a strong strike rate over time and we're upfront about every result.
How do I get the tips? +
No app, no account, no fuss. The moment you join, your private tips link lands on your phone by SMS — one tap and you're on this round's tips. Lost the text? Hit "My Tips" anywhere on the site and pop in your mobile number.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Absolutely. No lock-in contracts. Cancel whenever you like.
What happens in the off-season? +
When the NRL and AFL seasons wrap up, your subscription ends automatically — we don't bill you a cent over the off-season for tips that don't exist. Then before the new season kicks off, we'll text you so you can jump straight back in. Footy's back, den's open.
Is this legal? +
Yes. We provide tips and analysis for entertainment and information only — we're not a bookmaker and we don't take bets. You do your own betting through your own licensed bookmaker.
Do you guarantee I'll win money? +
No, and be wary of anyone who does. We provide a statistical edge based on data and analysis. Betting always carries risk. Never bet more than you can afford to lose.
