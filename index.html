FAQ

Questions, answered

How does the AI actually work? +
Our AI pipeline pulls in every NRL and AFL game — form, injuries, team news and live odds from four major bookmakers. It scores each game against our tipping rules to find genuine value, then a real punter reviews every tip before it's published.
When do tips get posted? +
Tips go out midweek, before the first games of the round kick off, so you've got time to get set. All paying members get them by SMS — straight to your phone, no app to download.
What happens when a tip loses? +
It goes on the record, same as the wins. No tipping service wins every bet — anyone who claims otherwise is lying. We aim for a strong strike rate over time and we're upfront about every result.
How do I get the tips? +
Straight to your phone by SMS — no app to download, no account to set up. You pay, you pop in your mobile number, and the tips land as a text each round. Simple as that.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Absolutely. No lock-in contracts. Cancel whenever you like.
Is this legal? +
Yes. We provide tips and analysis for entertainment and information only — we're not a bookmaker and we don't take bets. You do your own betting through your own licensed bookmaker.
Do you guarantee I'll win money? +
No, and be wary of anyone who does. We provide a statistical edge based on data and analysis. Betting always carries risk. Never bet more than you can afford to lose.
