How It Works

How we track the Scoreboard

Every number on this page is built from real tips, graded the same way, every week. Here's exactly how it works.

01

Flat staking

Every tip is exactly 1 unit — no chasing, no doubling up. A win returns the odds minus your stake; a loss is −1 unit. Same stake on every pick keeps the record honest and easy to follow.

02

Graded after every round

Once the games are done, each tip is marked win or lose at the exact odds we tipped. No quiet deleting of losers, no rewriting history — the result is the result.

03

The numbers up top

Win Rate = winning tips ÷ all decided tips. ROI = total profit ÷ total staked. Profit = your running return, shown in units and in dollars at $10 a unit. The graph plots that profit climbing (or dipping) tip by tip.

04

Filter it yourself

Tap a tile — 🎯 Singles, 🔗 Multis or 🚀 Hero — to filter the whole record by type, broken down by sport and round. Check our SGM strike rate, or how the AFL tips have gone — nothing's hidden.

A fair warning: past results are no guarantee of future returns. We aim for a strong edge over a full season — not a perfect record. Some weeks lose. That's betting. Bet responsibly.
