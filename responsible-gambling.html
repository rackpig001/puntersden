Our commitment

The Punters Den provides tips and analysis for information and entertainment only. We want betting to stay fun. Gambling should never be used to make money, recover losses, or escape stress — and it should never put your finances, relationships, or wellbeing at risk.

Always remember: There are no guaranteed winners. Our tips are a statistical edge, not a sure thing. Never bet more than you can comfortably afford to lose, and never chase your losses.

Tips for staying in control

  • Set a budget — decide what you can afford to lose before you start, and stick to it.
  • Set time limits — don't let betting take over your day.
  • Never chase losses — trying to win back money is how small problems become big ones.
  • Don't bet under the influence — or when stressed, upset, or bored.
  • Keep it social and fun — if it stops being enjoyable, take a break.
  • Bet with money, not credit — never borrow to gamble.

Signs it might be a problem

Gambling may be becoming harmful if you're spending more than you can afford, chasing losses, hiding your betting from others, feeling anxious or guilty about it, or it's affecting your work, mood, or relationships. If any of this sounds familiar, reaching out early makes a real difference.

Setting limits with your bookmaker

All licensed Australian betting operators offer responsible gambling tools — including deposit limits, activity statements, time-outs, and self-exclusion. Use them. They're there to help you stay in control.
